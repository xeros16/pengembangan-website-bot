# PIR Visuals v5.51.0 — Editorial Website Redesign

## Design direction
- Re-art-directed the public website around an editorial creative-network experience, taking structural inspiration from Bettr Academy's bold typography, section rhythm, varied content surfaces, and story-led presentation without copying its brand assets or content.
- Rebuilt the homepage hero's visual treatment for both themes.
- Light mode now uses warm ivory, soft stone and sage surfaces with dark charcoal typography; the hero creative index is a distinct ivory panel rather than dark text on dark green.
- Dark mode keeps a layered forest/charcoal palette with distinct section surfaces instead of a flat monochrome background.
- Refined navigation, buttons, card corners, spacing, section headings, category tiles, portfolio grid, manifesto, CTA and FAQ.
- Added mobile-specific editorial layout behavior and reduced-motion support.

## Important contrast fixes
- Explicit high-contrast overrides for hero heading, description, announcement, action links, statistics labels and values.
- Explicit color treatment for all major light-mode homepage sections, cards, metadata and footer.
- Replaced the prior dark hero treatment in light mode with a warm light hero surface and dark text.

## Compatibility
- No business logic, API, database schema, account, Baileys or game changes.
- Homepage CMS content, featured designers, carousel, portfolio items, category links, contact flow and Designer Studio links remain wired to existing data.
