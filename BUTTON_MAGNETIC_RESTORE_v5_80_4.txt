PIR Visuals v5.80.4 — Button mouse-following motion repair

Root cause addressed:
- v5.80.3 relied on the primary pointer media query and initial motionReduced snapshot.
- On hybrid devices or when motion was toggled after initial load, magnetic handlers could fail to initialize.
- GSAP hover handlers were attached only to controls present at initial page load.

Fix:
- Registers delegated pointer listeners independently of the primary-pointer media query and filters for mouse/pen pointer events.
- Delegated pointer events cover static and dynamically-rendered controls.
- Handler remains registered while respecting the live motion toggle and OS reduced-motion preference.
- Bounded magnetic movement: up to 10px horizontally and 8px vertically.
- Adds a small GSAP lift on hover and returns to center on pointer leave.
- Does not change category card hover palettes or page layout.
