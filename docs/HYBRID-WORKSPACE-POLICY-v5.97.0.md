# PIR Visuals Hybrid Operating Model (v5.97.0)

## Product boundary

### WhatsApp bot

The bot is a designer portfolio directory and direct-contact assistant only:
- discover designers and approved work;
- show public portfolio/profile information;
- expose only contact channels explicitly published by the designer;
- provide profile and portfolio status/analytics to the linked designer privately.

The bot does not create briefs, offers, orders, project rooms, checkout, payment, commission, escrow, transaction records, or disputes.

### Website

The website supports the public portfolio directory and may additionally provide an **optional** project workspace:
- direct WhatsApp contact remains the primary and simplest contact route;
- a designer may enable project inquiries to expose the optional workspace;
- the client may submit a brief and, if email delivery is configured, receive a one-time private workspace invitation;
- client and designer may record progress, messages, and feedback in the private workspace.

The workspace is a collaboration log only. It does not process payments, hold funds, calculate commissions, provide escrow, mediate a dispute, guarantee delivery, or become a party to the client-designer agreement.

## User-facing disclosures

Show a short disclosure before a brief is submitted and inside both sides of the workspace:

> Project workspace is optional. PIR Visuals only provides a private space to record the brief, progress, and feedback. Scope, price, contract, rights, revisions, delivery, and payment are agreed directly between the client and designer outside PIR Visuals.

## Data and privacy

- Client brief and contact details are private workspace data, not public portfolio content.
- Client workspace links are single-use and time-limited for initial activation; active sessions are private and expiring.
- Never post client brief content or private workspace updates into WhatsApp groups.
- Designers are responsible for only inviting the intended client and protecting their own account.
- Do not expose internal account JIDs as public designer contacts.

## Non-goals

No checkout, payment gateway, manual payment ledger, escrow, commission, dispute resolution, PIR-managed contract, order guarantee, or payment guarantee is part of this model.
