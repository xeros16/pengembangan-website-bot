# Privacy Policy – Pir Visuals

**Effective Date:** October 31, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Pir Visuals operates a WhatsApp bot that provides design-community tools and utilities.

This Privacy Policy explains the limited information handled by the bot when users use its Pinterest search shortcut. The shortcut only creates a public Pinterest search link from the keyword supplied in the command. The bot does not connect to the Pinterest API, scrape Pinterest pages, download Pinterest images, or collect Pinterest account information.

## 2. Information Handled

When a user runs `.pinsearch` or `.pinterest`, the bot processes the search keyword in the WhatsApp message to construct a Pinterest search URL. The bot does not send that keyword to Pinterest from its own server. The user opens the generated link in their own browser or Pinterest application.

The bot does not request or store Pinterest passwords, Pinterest access tokens, Pin IDs, or Pinterest image metadata for this feature.

## 3. Third-Party Services

When users open a Pinterest search link, Pinterest may collect information directly through its website or application under its own privacy policy:

- Pinterest Privacy Policy: https://policy.pinterest.com/en/privacy-policy

WhatsApp processes messages sent to and from the bot under its own terms and privacy policy:

- WhatsApp Privacy Policy: https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy

Pir Visuals does not control the independent data practices of these services.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

The Pinterest search shortcut does not maintain a Pinterest search history on behalf of users and does not store Pinterest API credentials.

Other bot features may process or store information needed to operate the WhatsApp community services. Those features should be reviewed according to their actual functionality and configuration.

## 5. Data Sharing

Pir Visuals does not sell personal information. The Pinterest search shortcut does not transmit search keywords to Pinterest from the bot's server. Users choose whether to open the generated external link.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect bot configuration, WhatsApp session files, databases, and operational logs. Users should not send passwords or private credentials through bot commands.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. User Requests

For questions about this Privacy Policy or requests concerning information handled by Pir Visuals, contact the administrator using the contact information below.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change. The latest version should be published at the same URL.
