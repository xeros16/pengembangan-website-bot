PIR Visuals v5.80.5 — Checkbox and verification form spacing fix

Changes are limited to the two reported UI issues:
- Checkbox and radio controls are explicitly sized to 18×18px and no longer inherit the generic 44px form-field minimum height or 11px padding.
- Discovery filter labels keep their checkbox and text on one aligned horizontal row.
- Resend verification email input and button are placed in a responsive grid with a 12px gap; on narrow screens the button moves below the input.
- Existing magnetic pointer-following animation and GSAP behavior are retained unchanged.
